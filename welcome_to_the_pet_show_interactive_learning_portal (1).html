Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) tools were utilised during the creation of the Pet Show website to assist with producing visual illustrations, developing educational materials and designing interactive learning activities.

All AI-generated and AI-assisted resources were checked, refined and adjusted to ensure their suitability for Year 1 pupils and their relevance to the English language learning objectives.

AI served as a supplementary resource throughout the development process. The final selection of learning content, activities and educational approaches was determined by the website creator.

Credits, Privacy & Responsible Use

Educational Purpose

Welcome to the Pet Show! is an interactive digital learning environment designed to introduce Year 1 pupils to common pet vocabulary and the use of prepositions of place in English.

Copyright and Credits

Educational videos embedded from YouTube belong to their respective content creators and are used through the platform's embedding feature. Illustrations and interactive learning materials created with AI assistance were prepared for educational purposes. Any third-party resources used are acknowledged through their original sources.

Privacy and Data Protection

The interactive activities are designed to function without requiring pupils to enter personal details or sensitive information. Responses and quiz results are processed within the webpage and are not intentionally shared with external assessment databases.

However, third-party services such as Google Sites and YouTube may collect or process certain user information in accordance with their own privacy policies and data protection practices.

Responsible Digital Use

Pupils are encouraged to interact with the website appropriately, respect the work and contributions of others, and protect their personal information when using digital platforms. They should also seek assistance from their teachers or parents whenever necessary.

Educational Disclaimer

The Pet Show website is intended solely as an educational resource to support young learners in developing their English vocabulary, listening skills and understanding of basic prepositions. Its content is designed to complement classroom learning and does not replace guidance from teachers.

👩‍🏫 Lesson Guide

Teacher's Corner

Year 1 English Lesson Plan – Pet Show (60 Minutes)

A. Lesson Information

Subject English
Year Year 1
Duration 60 minutes
Theme World of Knowledge
Topic Pet Show
Main Skill Speaking
Complementary Skill Listening
Language Focus Pet vocabulary and prepositions of place
Grammar / Vocabulary IN, ON, UNDER; cat, dog, rabbit, bird, fish, hamster
Teaching Materials Interactive Google Site website, laptop, LCD projector, speakers and webcam

B. Content and Learning Standards

Main Skill: Speaking

  • Content Standard 2.1: Communicate simple information intelligibly.
  • Learning Standard 2.1.5: Describe people and objects using suitable words and phrases. (learning standards)

Complementary Skill: Listening

  • Content Standard 1.2: Understand meaning in a variety of familiar contexts.
  • Learning Standard 1.2.5: Understand a wide range of short supported questions. (learning standards)

C. Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to:

  1. Name at least four pets using appropriate English vocabulary with intelligible pronunciation.
  2. Describe the positions of pets using the prepositions IN, ON and UNDER by correctly answering at least 4 out of 5 questions.

D. Success Criteria

Pupils can:

  1. Sing the pet vocabulary chant and correctly pronounce at least four pet names.
  2. Select the correct preposition (IN, ON or UNDER) when answering questions about the positions of pets.
  3. Participate in the Pet Grab and Throw game by placing pets in the correct positions.

E. Teaching and Learning Activities

1. Set Induction – Welcome to the Pet Show!

⏱ 5 minutes
  1. Teacher displays the Google Sites homepage on the LCD projector.
  2. Teacher welcomes the pupils and introduces the topic by saying, "Hello, little animal lovers! Are you ready for our Pet Show?"
  3. Pupils look at the different pet pictures displayed on the screen.
  4. Teacher asks, "What animals can you see?" and encourages pupils to name the pets they already know.
  5. Pupils respond by naming the animals using simple English words.
  6. Teacher encourages pupils to pronounce the names clearly and asks follow-up questions such as, "Do you have a pet?" and "What is your favourite animal?"
WEBSITE SECTION: HOME – WELCOME TO THE PET SHOW!

2. Presentation – Meet the Pets and Learn

⏱ 15 minutes
  1. Teacher navigates to the Meet the Pets section and plays the YouTube video, Pets Vocabulary Chant for Children.
  2. Pupils watch and listen to the chant.
  3. Teacher encourages pupils to sing along and pronounce the names of the pets.
  4. Teacher introduces the six pets (cat, dog, rabbit, bird, fish and hamster) using the pictures and audio pronunciation buttons.
  5. Pupils repeat the names of the pets after listening to the audio.
  6. Teacher asks pupils to identify the pets individually and encourages them to say the names without relying on the audio.
  7. Teacher moves to the Learn section and introduces the prepositions IN, ON and UNDER using visual illustrations.
  8. Teacher displays the sentences:
    • The cat is IN the box.
    • The bird is ON the tree.
    • The dog is UNDER the table.
  9. Pupils listen and repeat the sentences after the teacher.
  10. Teacher asks questions such as, "Where is the cat?" and "Where is the bird?"
  11. Pupils answer orally using the correct prepositions and complete sentences.
WEBSITE SECTIONS: MEET THE PETS + LEARN

3. Practice – Let's Explore!

⏱ 15 minutes
  1. Teacher navigates to the Let's Explore section and introduces the interactive activities.
  2. Pupils participate in the Guess the Pet activity by answering picture-based questions.
  3. Teacher encourages pupils to answer using complete sentences, such as "It is a rabbit."
  4. Teacher proceeds to the Where Is the Pet? activity and displays pictures of pets in different positions.
  5. Pupils identify the positions of the pets and answer orally using IN, ON or UNDER.
  6. Pupils complete the sentences by choosing the correct missing prepositions.
  7. Teacher invites selected pupils to explain their answers using simple sentences.
  8. Teacher discusses the answers and provides guidance when necessary.
WEBSITE SECTION: LET'S EXPLORE!

4. Production – Pet Grab and Throw

⏱ 15 minutes
  1. Teacher opens the Pet Grab and Throw game and demonstrates how to use hand gestures in front of the laptop camera.
  2. Teacher explains that pupils need to grab a pet using their hand and throw it towards the correct position shown on the screen.
  3. Teacher demonstrates an example by saying, "I will grab the cat and throw it IN the box."
  4. Pupils take turns participating in the game while their classmates observe and help identify the correct positions.
  5. Pupils complete challenges such as:
    • Grab the cat and throw it IN the box.
    • Grab the bird and throw it ON the tree.
    • Grab the dog and throw it UNDER the table.
  6. Before or after each attempt, pupils are encouraged to describe their actions using complete sentences.
  7. The game provides immediate feedback and awards points for correct answers.
  8. Teacher reinforces the correct use of the prepositions and encourages pupils to speak clearly throughout the activity.
WEBSITE SECTION: LET'S PLAY! – PET GRAB AND THROW

5. Closure – Let's Check!

⏱ 10 minutes
  1. Teacher asks pupils to recall the six pets and the three prepositions learnt during the lesson.
  2. Pupils respond by naming the pets and saying IN, ON and UNDER.
  3. Teacher asks selected pupils to describe the positions of pets using simple sentences.
  4. Teacher navigates to the Let's Check section and displays the digital assessment.
  5. Pupils answer the multiple-choice questions individually or together as a class, depending on the available devices.
  6. Teacher discusses selected questions and encourages pupils to explain their answers orally.
  7. The website automatically marks the answers and displays the final score.
  8. Teacher congratulates pupils on their participation and briefly summarises what they have learnt.
WEBSITE SECTION: LET'S CHECK!

F. Assessment

Assessment method Evidence of learning
Observation Pupils participate in the pet vocabulary chant and pronounce pet names.
Oral questioning Pupils answer questions about pet positions.
Interactive activities Pupils select appropriate prepositions in Let's Explore.
Pet Grab and Throw Pupils demonstrate their understanding of IN, ON and UNDER through the game.
Digital assessment Pupils answer the multiple-choice questions and achieve at least 4/5 correct responses in the targeted position questions.
